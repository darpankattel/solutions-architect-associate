## Amazon Fraud Detector
Designed for detecting fraudulent online activities (e.g., fake signups, payment fraud).

Not designed to scan or classify S3 data for PII.

## Amazon WorkDocs
WorkDocs is for collaboration and document storage, not for serving high-quality public content. It’s not suitable as a replacement for S3 in this use case.

## AWS Network Firewall
Provides traffic flow inspection and traffic filtering.

Stateful firewall: tracks connections and enforces protocols and domain filtering.

Intrusion Prevention System (IPS): blocks exploit attempts.

Web filtering: stops access to malicious or unauthorized URLs.
